FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding booking confirmation, power backups, water filtration, or contractor group rates? Find answers below.

How do I confirm my booking at Reeme Guest House? add

After submitting the reservation form above, you will receive an instant summary reference. You can dispatch it via WhatsApp directly to Manager Didintle Thupae for instant confirmation.

Do you accommodate mining contractor teams & long stays? add

Yes! We specialize in corporate housing contracts, monthly contractor rate vouchers, packed daily lunches, and vehicle registration for automated perimeter gates.

Are hot water and electricity guaranteed during load shedding? add

Absolutely. The establishment is outfitted with dual high-capacity solar geysers and back-up systems ensuring uninterrupted hot water and lighting 24/7.

Is tap water safe and filtered on site? add

Yes. All water supplied on the property passes through an industrial multi-stage water filtration and softening cylinder for pure, safe drinking water.

What are the check-in and check-out times? add

Standard check-in is between 14:00 and 22:00 daily. Check-out is at 10:00 AM. Late check-ins can be arranged in advance with management.

What meal plans are available? add

Options include Daily Warm Breakfast, Full Board (Breakfast, Packed Worksite Lunch, & Dinner), Self-Catering Kitchen Access, or Corporate Food Vouchers.
