Who We Are — Tailored Lodging in Northern Cape

Welcome to Reeme Guest House. We are an established guesthouse dedicated to providing a true home-away-from-home experience. Whether you are a corporate contractor working in the area, a visiting engineer, or a family on holiday, our establishment offers a safe, peaceful, and personal stay.

Managed by Didintle Thupae, our management team coordinates directly with procurement partners and mining houses across Postmasburg to simplify short-term and long-term stays with complete meal voucher support, daily maid service, and 24/7 security.
