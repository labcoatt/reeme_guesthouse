About Reeme Guest House

Our Story & Pillars

Dedicated to providing a high-end contractor-friendly and family-friendly home away from home in Postmasburg.

history_edu Who We Are

Welcome to Reeme Guest House. We are a young established guesthouse dedicated to providing a home-away-from-home experience. Whether you are a corporate contractor working in the area, a family on holiday, or a couple on a weekend retreat, we exist to offer a safe, comfortable, and personal stay tailored to your needs.

Our management team, led by Didintle Thupae, works closely with mining houses and engineering houses in the Northern Cape to accommodate teams for short or long projects. We coordinate directly with procurement partners to simplify contractor stays, offering tailored packages and complete comfort tools.

Advanced Secure Premises

Your peace of mind is our absolute priority. Our lodge features 24-hour high-definition security camera monitoring, electric boundary fencing, security motion sensors, panic buttons, and walled parking. Rest easy knowing your vehicles and equipment are fully secured.
